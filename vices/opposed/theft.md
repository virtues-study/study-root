# Theft

![Vice](https://img.shields.io/badge/type-vice-red)
![Justice](https://img.shields.io/badge/family-justice-white)

## Definition

Theft is the unjust taking or use of another person's property without the owner's consent. Aquinas distinguishes theft from robbery chiefly by the manner in which the property is taken: theft is secret whereas robbery involves violence.

The wrong of theft lies in acting against the right of another to possess and control what belongs to that person. For this reason theft is especially connected with commutative justice, which governs equality and what is due in dealings between individuals.

Aquinas also notes that judgments about theft depend upon questions of ownership, necessity, and the proper use of external goods. The moral category therefore concerns unjust taking rather than every instance of using something that another person possesses.

**Primary reference:** *Summa Theologiae* II-II, q.66.
