# Commutative Justice

![Virtue](https://img.shields.io/badge/type-virtue-blue)
![Justice](https://img.shields.io/badge/family-justice-white)

## Definition

Commutative justice concerns dealings between one person and another and seeks equality in exchanges and transactions by rendering to each person what is due.

**Primary reference:** *Summa Theologiae* II-II, q.61, aa.1-2.
