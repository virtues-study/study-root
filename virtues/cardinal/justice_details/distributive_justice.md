# Distributive Justice

![Virtue](https://img.shields.io/badge/type-virtue-blue)
![Justice](https://img.shields.io/badge/family-justice-white)

## Definition

Distributive justice concerns the relation of the community as a whole to its members and governs the proportionate distribution of common goods among them.

**Primary reference:** *Summa Theologiae* II-II, q.61, aa.1-2.
