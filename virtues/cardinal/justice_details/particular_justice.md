# Particular Justice

![Virtue](https://img.shields.io/badge/type-virtue-blue)
![Justice](https://img.shields.io/badge/family-justice-white)

## Definition

Particular justice concerns what is due to particular persons rather than the ordering of a person's actions toward the common good as a whole. It is divided into distributive justice and commutative justice.

**Primary references:** *Summa Theologiae* II-II, q.58, a.7; q.61, a.1.
