# General Justice

![Virtue](https://img.shields.io/badge/type-virtue-blue)
![Justice](https://img.shields.io/badge/family-justice-white)

## Definition

General justice, also called legal justice, orders the actions of a person in relation to the common good. Aquinas calls it general because it can direct the acts of the other virtues toward the good of the community.

**Primary reference:** *Summa Theologiae* II-II, q.58, aa.5-6.
