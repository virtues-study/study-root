# Liberality

![Virtue](https://img.shields.io/badge/type-virtue-blue)
![Justice](https://img.shields.io/badge/family-justice-white)

## Definition

Liberality is the virtue that orders a person's relationship to material possessions, especially money, so that wealth is neither loved nor retained inordinately and can be given fittingly when circumstances call for it.

Aquinas distinguishes liberality from justice in the strict sense. Justice gives another what already belongs or is strictly due to that person; liberality gives appropriately from what belongs to the giver.

Nevertheless, liberality is annexed to justice because both virtues concern external goods and are directed toward others. Liberality concerns a moral due based upon fittingness rather than the strict legal debt characteristic of justice.

The liberal person is therefore not simply someone who gives large amounts. Virtue requires giving to the right persons, for the right reasons, in the right manner, while maintaining a properly ordered attitude toward possessions.

**Primary reference:** *Summa Theologiae* II-II, q.117.
